The Transition

"The work that wouldn't leave my head wasn't the code. It was the question behind the code."

I build production AI workflows, GTM automation systems, and analytics pipelines. I'm just as comfortable presenting findings to leadership as I am debugging a Make orchestration that's failing silently at step 4. I started as a software engineer, kept noticing patterns that didn't add up, and eventually realized the question behind the system mattered more than the system itself. That's still how I work.
